My [notes page](/notes/) was inspired by Jim Neilson's [notes site](https://notes.jim-nielsen.com/).

I'm using it differently, but maybe it'll inspire someone else to make a web page on their site for small posts that don't *quite* feel like a large enough comment for a whole post on the blog.

Jim uses his notes site specifically for reading notes, but I'm using mine as a way to quickly get thoughts out without refining. Reducing friction has already made it easier to share more consistently.
